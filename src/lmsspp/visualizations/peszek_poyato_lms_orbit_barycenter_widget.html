Peszek-Poyato barycenters on a finite-N LMS canonical orbit

The radius slider is an initialization hyperparameter: it first generates an observed cloud x_i^0 with the requested exact-gauge radius, then the widget solves sum_i a_i M_{-w_*}(x_i^0)=0, freezes p_i^0=M_{-w_*}(x_i^0), and draws the reduced trajectory w(t). Click the plot to evaluate the Peszek-Poyato W^alpha kernel barycenter of the selected empirical support at the probe point x.

show comparison cloud
b_alpha(x)=E[|x-Z|^{-alpha} Z]/E[|x-Z|^{-alpha}], with Z chosen by the PP-support selector.
