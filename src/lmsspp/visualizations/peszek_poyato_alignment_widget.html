Interpretation

For α = 0, the entire distribution collapses to the barycenter: A₀(x)=x-m. For 0 < α < 1, the force is no longer determined only by the barycenter. It points toward the probe-dependent weighted barycenter bα(x), and its local precision / anisotropy is encoded by Hα(x).
